# Hyo Life OS User Guide

## Daily Flow

Hyo Life OS is designed to protect the order of attention:

```text
Radar -> Vision -> Today -> Strategy -> Evolve
```

The app opens with sensing and orientation, but the real daily gate is `Today`.

1. Open `Today`.
2. Complete the `Morning Gate` 60-second check-in.
3. Run the TOP 3 and core habit loop.
4. Use `Strategy` only after the morning check-in, so planning does not replace execution.
5. Use `Evolve` for weekly review, backups, security, and Grand Plan edits.

## Why Strategy And Evolve May Be Locked

`Strategy` and `Evolve` are intentionally locked until the Morning Gate is complete when `아침 집중 잠금` is on.

Reason:

- Planning and system editing are powerful, but they can become avoidance.
- The app forces one short execution-oriented check-in before opening deeper planning surfaces.
- This keeps the system from becoming a dashboard that feels productive but does not move the day.

How to unlock:

1. Go to `Today`.
2. Fill energy, mode, and today's pledge.
3. Click `체크인 완료`.

How to disable the lock:

1. Go to `Evolve`.
2. In `Goal Update Center`, turn off `아침 집중 잠금`.

If the lock is already active and you need `Evolve`, complete the Morning Gate first. That is the intended path.

## Core Screens

- `Radar`: external signals, method update candidates, idea pipeline, skill forge.
- `Vision`: identity direction, Ikigai, Transurfing-style importance reduction, operating doctrine.
- `Today`: Morning Gate, TOP 3, habits, Flow Matrix, Buddhist log, journal, coach brief.
- `Strategy`: Grand Plan, WOOP, Mandarat, gaeun priorities, resource design.
- `Evolve`: weekly review, trend bars, settings, backups, lock, deploy checklist, goal updates.

## Transurfing Panel

The `중요도 낮추기` panel is not meant to be motivational decoration. It is a working rule set:

- Drop excess importance.
- Stop gripping the outcome.
- Keep the inner slide, but pay with calm action.
- Treat obstacles as course correction, not prophecy.
- Return to the next small move when attachment rises.

This keeps desire from becoming pressure and pressure from becoming self-sabotage.
